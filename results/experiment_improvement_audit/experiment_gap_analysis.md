# Experiment Gap Analysis

- **Phase 2F Temporal Stress**: Manuscript-Ready. Proves SLA limits hold under sustained stress.
- **Phase 3C HoF Sweep**: Manuscript-Ready as an Ablation Study. Proves Pareto HoF admission outperforms random, but overall replay degrades plasticity.
- **Phase 3D Co-evolution (Limited-seed)**: Limited-seed. Sufficient for trend identification, but 10 generations (vs 20) with 3 seeds means wide confidence intervals. Claiming $p<0.05$ robustness requires a full 20x5 run.
- **Adaptive HoF Diagnostic**: Preliminary. Shows whether adaptive probability controls can salvage HoF performance.
- **Heuristic Baselines**: Manuscript-Ready. Provides strong operational anchoring points for the DRL models.
